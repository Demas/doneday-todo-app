## Why the data is used

DoneDay uses data to provide guest storage, optional account access, synchronization, reminders selected by the person, support, security, and legal compliance. DoneDay does not sell task, birthday, or account data.

## Choices and controls

- A person can use DoneDay in guest mode without an account.
- A person can export JSON or CSV data from Settings.
- An authenticated person can request account and cloud-data deletion from the account panel.
- Browser, operating-system, and device settings control notification, alarm, and push permissions.
